About

An example of a server-rendered site in Rust.

  • actix-web answers the requests ("src/routes.rs").
  • askama turns the templates in "templates/" into Rust when the project compiles, so a typo in a template is a compile error, not a broken page.
  • htmx fetches pages and parts of pages in the background, so the site feels quick without a JavaScript framework. Every page still works without it.

Things to try: follow a link and watch the network tab; search as you type; add a book with a field left empty; remove a book from the list.
